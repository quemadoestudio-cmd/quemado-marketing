# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-06-07*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Quemado Studio is a strategy-first digital marketing agency that helps small businesses go from invisible to unmissable online.
**What it does:** We develop and execute full-spectrum digital marketing strategies for small businesses — combining social media management, content creation (video + photo), web design, branding, graphic design, and product strategy into a coherent growth plan. We start with strategy so every execution decision has a reason behind it.
**Product category:** Digital marketing agency / creative studio
**Product type:** Service business (retainer + project-based)
**Business model:** Monthly retainers for ongoing services (social media, content, strategy); one-time project fees for web design, branding, and campaigns.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Small businesses — local shops, service providers, restaurants, entrepreneurs, and growing brands that have a real product or service but little to no online presence.
**Decision-makers:** Business owner / founder (usually the same person). Occasionally a marketing coordinator at a slightly larger small business.
**Primary use case:** Getting found online and building a credible, active digital presence from scratch or reviving a neglected one.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Help me look as good online as I do in person"
- "Turn my social media into something that actually brings in customers"
- "Tell me what to focus on — I don't have time to figure this out myself"
**Use cases:**
- New business launching its first website and social presence
- Established local business that has never invested in digital marketing
- Business owner burned by a previous agency that produced pretty work but no results

|---------|-------------|-----------|------------------|

| Growth-stage small biz | Getting more leads, standing out locally | Tried ads or social before and wasted money | Strategy first so budget isn't burned on guesswork |

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Small businesses are invisible online — no strategy, inconsistent presence, and no idea what's working or why.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Freelancers are cheap but scattered — no strategy, just execution
- Big agencies are expensive and treat small clients as low priority
- DIY is possible but the owner doesn't have time or expertise
- Generic content mills produce posts that look fine but don't reflect the brand or convert

**What it costs them:** Lost customers who found a competitor online instead. Ad spend wasted on campaigns with no strategy behind them. Time spent managing disconnected freelancers.
**Emotional tension:** Embarrassment at having a weak online presence. Frustration at spending money on marketing that didn't work. Anxiety about being left behind while competitors grow online.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local freelancers and boutique agencies — fall short because they skip strategy and jump straight to execution; clients don't understand what they're getting or why.
**Secondary:** DIY tools (Canva, Wix, Meta Ads Manager) — fall short because the owner runs out of time, consistency drops, and there's no strategy tying it together.
**Indirect:** Word-of-mouth and referrals only — falls short because it doesn't scale and leaves the business invisible to anyone outside their existing network.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Strategy comes first — every service is grounded in a clear positioning and growth logic before any creative work begins
- Full-stack creative + marketing in one studio — web, branding, content, social, and strategy without stitching together multiple vendors
- Real content (video + photo) — not generic stock or AI filler; original assets that reflect the actual brand

**How we do it differently:** We run a structured intake and strategy phase before touching any deliverable. Clients leave with a clear picture of who they're talking to, what they're saying, and why — then we execute against that.
**Why that's better:** Clients stop wasting money on tactics that don't connect. Every piece of content, every ad, every post has a job to do.
**Why customers choose us:** They want someone who will think for them, not just execute for them — and they want work that looks like them, not like a template.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We tried marketing before and it didn't work" | That's usually because execution happened without strategy. We start with a plan so every dollar and hour has a clear goal. |
| "We can't afford an agency" | We work with small business budgets. The question isn't whether you can afford marketing — it's whether you can afford to stay invisible. |
| "We can do social media ourselves" | You can, but will you? Consistency is the hard part. We handle it so you don't have to think about it. |

**Anti-persona:** Businesses looking for the cheapest option with no interest in strategy. Clients who want to micromanage every post. Companies that need enterprise-scale paid media management.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with inconsistent freelancers, wasted ad budget, or a website that looks outdated and generates zero leads.
**Pull:** A studio that handles strategy AND execution in one place — no need to manage multiple vendors or explain context repeatedly.
**Habit:** Relying on word-of-mouth and referrals because "that's always worked." Posting on social only when they remember.
**Anxiety:** "What if we invest and still don't see results?" / "Will they understand our industry?" / "Is this going to take a lot of my time?"
